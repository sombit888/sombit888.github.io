We build SPEAR-VLM, a 3D-aware vision-language model that infers object coordinates from a single image, and use it to train SPEAR-1, a robotic foundation model with grounded 3D perception and language-conditioned control. SPEAR-1 trains on ~45M frames from 24 Open X-Embodiment datasets and matches or surpasses π0-FAST and π0.5 while using 20× fewer robot demonstrations. Model weights are openly released.
