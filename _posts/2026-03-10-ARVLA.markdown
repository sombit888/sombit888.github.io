We replace the chunk-based action head of a VLA with a standalone autoregressive action expert that keeps a long-lived memory of its own actions while attending to vision-language features that refresh asynchronously. AR-VLA produces smoother trajectories, remembers task state that is no longer visible, and matches or beats chunk-based VLAs in SIMPLER and on a real WidowX robot.
